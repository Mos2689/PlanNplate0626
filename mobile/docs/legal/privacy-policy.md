## 1. What this policy covers
This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By creating an account or using PlanNplate, you agree to the handling of your information as described here.

## 2. Information we collect

**a. Account information**
- Your name and email address.
- Authentication identifiers when you sign in with email, Apple, Google or Facebook (we receive a unique ID and basic profile info from the provider; we do not receive your social media password).

**b. Cooking profile & preferences**
- Household size, cooking days, weeknight time, skill level, kitchen equipment, cuisine preferences, budget, pantry staples and goals.
- Meal habits (e.g. cook / leftovers / buy).

**c. Dietary restrictions and allergies (sensitive information)**
- If you choose to tell us about allergies or dietary restrictions, this may be **sensitive information** under the Privacy Act. We collect it **only with your consent**, which you give when you enter it during onboarding or in your preferences, and we use it **solely** to filter and validate recipes and meal plans for you. You can edit or remove it at any time, and you are never required to provide it.

**d. Content you create**
- Meal plans, saved and generated recipes, imported recipes, grocery lists, cooking logs and recipe ratings.

**e. Voice input**
- If you use the "Speak" feature, we record short audio, send it to be transcribed to text, and use the text to add dishes or grocery items. We do not use your voice recordings for any other purpose.

**f. Purchases & subscriptions**
- Subscription status and entitlements. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we do **not** receive or store your full payment card details. Subscription state is managed through our payments provider (RevenueCat).

**g. Usage & device information**
- App interactions and events (for analytics and product improvement), app version, device type and operating system, and general technical/diagnostic data.

**h. Images**
- Recipe images sourced from stock providers, AI-generated images, images embedded in content you import, and any images you add.

## 3. How we use your information
We use your information to:
- create and manage your account;
- personalise recipes, meal plans and grocery lists;
- generate, parse and validate recipes using AI (see section 5);
- process subscriptions and provide premium features;
- operate, secure, debug and improve the app;
- respond to your requests and send you service communications.

We **do not sell** your personal information, and we **do not use your content to train third-party AI models**.

## 4. Legal bases / consent
We collect and use personal information where it is reasonably necessary for our functions and activities, and with your consent where required (including for sensitive information such as allergies). Where you have provided consent, you may withdraw it at any time by editing your preferences or deleting your account.

## 5. AI processing
To generate recipes, suggest meal plans, and extract recipes from text/links, relevant inputs (such as your preferences and the content you provide or import) are sent through our servers to our AI provider (OpenAI) and returned to the app. This processing is used only to provide the feature you requested. AI output can be inaccurate — see our Terms and the in-app disclaimers regarding AI-generated recipes.

## 6. Who we share information with (service providers)
We share information with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, only to provide their service to us. These currently include:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Authentication, database, storage and secure server functions |
| OpenAI | AI recipe generation, parsing and extraction |
| RevenueCat | Subscription management |
| PostHog | Product analytics |
| Meta | App events / measurement |
| Pexels | Stock recipe imagery |
| Apple / Google | Sign-in and in-app purchases |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

## 7. Overseas disclosure
Some of our service providers store or process data outside Australia, including in the **United States**. Where we disclose personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the APPs.

## 8. Storage and security
Your data is stored with reputable providers and encrypted in transit. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data retention
We keep your personal information while your account is active and as needed to provide the app. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to meet legal, accounting or security obligations.

## 10. Your rights and choices
You can:
- **Access and correct** your information — most of it is editable in the app;
- **Delete your account and data** from within your profile;
- **Turn off the microphone or notifications** in your device settings;
- **Withdraw consent** for sensitive information by removing it or deleting your account;
- **Ask us** to access, correct or delete information by emailing **privacy@heylivingclub.com**.

We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with the Privacy Act.

## 11. Complaints
If you have a privacy concern, contact us first at **privacy@heylivingclub.com**. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at **oaic.gov.au**.

## 12. Children
