## 3. The personal information we collect

### 3.1 Information you give us

- **Account information:** your email address, password, and display name. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider — we never see or store your plaintext password.
- **Dietary and health preferences:** allergies, intolerances, and dietary restrictions (such as vegetarian, halal, or gluten-free). This is treated as sensitive/health information and collected only with your consent (see Section 5).
- **Cooking preferences:** preferred cuisines, cooking time, skill level, household size, servings, weekly and monthly budget, and preferred units.
- **Content you create:** meal plans, saved and imported recipes, grocery lists, cooking logs, and meal ratings and feedback.
- **Voice input:** short audio recordings when you use the optional “speak your grocery list” feature (see Section 3.4).
- **Imported recipe links:** URLs you paste to import a recipe, and the recipe content returned.
- **Communications:** the content of messages you send us.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data:** the features you use and related counts (such as the number of meal plans generated, grocery lists built, or voice entries used), which we use to operate free and premium limits and to improve the App.
- **Device and technical data:** device and operating-system type, app version, approximate region/locale derived from your device settings (used to select measurement units and currency), and diagnostic or crash information.
- **Subscription data:** subscription status, entitlements, and purchase history received from the app stores via our subscription provider. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

### 3.3 Information from third parties

We receive transaction and subscription-validation data from Apple and Google when you make an in-app purchase, and subscription status from our subscription provider. We do not buy personal information from data brokers.

### 3.4 Voice data

When you use the optional voice-to-grocery feature, the App accesses your device microphone (only after you grant permission) to record a short clip while you speak. The audio is sent securely to our backend and forwarded to a speech-to-text provider to convert it into text. We keep the resulting grocery-list items; the audio recording is not retained on our servers after transcription. You can always type your list instead of using voice.
