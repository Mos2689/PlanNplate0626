# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home cooks, families, solo diners, and budget-conscious people who want to plan meals, manage recipes, and turn those plans into practical grocery lists with less effort.

## Product Purpose

PlanNplate helps people move from food preferences to an achievable weekly meal plan, useful recipes, and a consolidated grocery list. Success means users feel confident about what to cook and can complete planning quickly without losing control of dietary needs, cost, or serving size.

## Brand Personality

Warm, capable, and quietly delightful. The experience should feel editorial and food-led while remaining familiar enough to use without explanation.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic health-app minimalism, loud wellness gradients, dense enterprise UI, novelty interactions that obscure standard controls, and decorative effects that compete with food imagery or the user's task.

## Design Principles

- Keep the food and the next useful action visually dominant.
- Make complex planning feel calm through progressive disclosure and clear states.
- Earn delight through responsive details, warmth, and haptics rather than decoration.
- Preserve familiar mobile affordances and consistent component behavior.
- Treat dietary, allergy, account, and payment states with clarity and care.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, meaningful accessibility labels, comfortable touch targets, non-color-only state cues, dark-mode support, international names, and reduced reliance on motion. Core tasks must remain understandable with screen readers and system accessibility settings.
